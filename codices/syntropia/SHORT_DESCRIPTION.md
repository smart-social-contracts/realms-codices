# Syntropia

A full-featured representative democracy modelled on modern western states. Power is separated into three branches — a parliament that debates and votes on laws, an executive that approves or vetoes them, and a judiciary that can strike down unconstitutional laws. Citizens join through verified identity (zero-knowledge passport proof) and gain access to universal welfare (healthcare, unemployment, pension), progressive taxation, and an annual government budget split across departments. The realm can lease physical territory from a host country through formal treaties, license private companies to deliver public services (hospitals, schools, security, courts), and organise its land into geographic zones. The entire realm follows a lifecycle from early sign-up phase through full operation to eventual wind-down.
